Who we are

Helping vets reach the services they earned.

ABQVETS is built to connect veterans with the resources that are otherwise buried behind confusing websites, broken listings, or paywalls. We walk through the internet maze so the not-so-savvy user can find help without the overwhelm.

Every link, hotline, and organization on this site has been hand-picked because it solves a real problem for someone who may not spend hours cataloguing benefits. We keep the tone direct, the navigation simple, and the updates frequent.

Why we keep it simple

Veterans deserve direct access, not jargon. We organize resources by need, use plain language, and spell out how to start a request, who to call, and what documentation matters.

  • Curated guides that match everyday questions (“Where do I send my VA claim?”).
  • Local and national contacts so help stays close to home regardless of online ability.
  • Step-by-step next actions for navigating healthcare, housing, jobs, and crisis support.

Built for the not-so-savvy internet user

No endless menus, no vague links. We mirror what someone would ask a buddy for — “Where is the quickest way to reset myVA password?” — then answer it in a short paragraph and a working link.
