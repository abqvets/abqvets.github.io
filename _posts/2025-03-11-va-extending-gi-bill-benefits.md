The VA is extending GI Bill benefits. The Rudisill Supreme Court Decision is allowing additional benefits for certain veterans. As of April 16th, 2024, veterans can get up to a year of additional education benefits if you qualify. Check out the eligibility requirements and application [Here](https://benefits.va.gov/gibill/rudisill.asp#:~:text=On%20April%2016%2C%202024%20the,receive%20additional%20GI%20Bill%20benefits).
