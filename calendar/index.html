NM Department of Veterans' Services Bataan Memorial Building, Eternal Flame @ Bataan Memorial Building, 407 Galisteo St, Santa Fe, NM 87501, USA

Annual Bataan Remembrance Ceremony April 9th

Each year on April 9th, New Mexicans gather to honor one of the most significant chapters in the state’s military history during the Bataan Remembrance Ceremony, presented by the New Mexico National Guard in partnership with the New Mexico Department of Veterans’ Services. The ceremony commemorates the 1,800 soldiers of the New Mexico National Guard’s 200th and 515th Coast Artillery Regiments stationed in the Philippines on Dec. 8, 1941, when Japan launched a surprise attack just hours after Pearl Harbor. Originally deployed for training, these New Mexicans soon became part of a combined American and Filipino force defending the Bataan Peninsula against Japanese invasion. After four months of intense fighting, Allied forces, outnumbered and running out of food and supplies, surrendered on April 9, 1942. Thousands of American and Filipino troops were taken prisoner and subjected to the brutal Bataan Death March. The ceremony will take place before the eternal flame at the Bataan Memorial Building on Thursday, April 9th, at 11:00 am, 407 Galisteo Street, Santa Fe, NM 87501 .

NM Department of Veterans' Services Hotel Encanto de Las Cruces @ 705 S. Telshor Blvd., Las Cruces, NM 88011

NMDVS Annual Veterans Conference in Las Cruces

The New Mexico Department of Veterans’ Services (NMDVS) will host its annual 2026 Veterans Conference on April 17th–18th at Hotel Encanto de Las Cruces in Las Cruces, 705 S. Telshor Blvd., Las Cruces, NM 88011, bringing together Veterans, families, service providers, and advocates from across the state. This year’s theme, “Empowering Every Veteran, Enriching Every Community,” highlights the strong partnership between New Mexico’s Veteran community and the many organizations dedicated to serving those who have worn the uniform. The two-day conference will feature program briefings from state, federal, and community partners who support Veterans, along with updates on available services, resources, and state and federal benefits. Attendees will have opportunities to connect directly with Veteran-friendly organizations and learn more about programs designed to assist Veterans and their families. Veterans will have the opportunity to meet directly with NMDVS staff and service providers to get help with benefits applications and claims questions. Support will be available for property tax waivers and exemptions, obtaining the New Mexico State Parks Lifetime Veteran Pass, filing VA benefits and compensation claims, and answering questions about VA health care eligibility and enrollment. Participants are encouraged to bring a copy of their DD-214 (if applicable), their VA award letter, and any VA correspondence related to benefits to help streamline the process. The conference will take place on Friday, April 17, from 8:00 a.m. to 4:15 p.m ., and Saturday, April 18, from 8:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. Registration is free and open to Veterans and their families, but space is limited to 205 participants. NM Department of Veterans' Services Sonoma Ranch Golf Course @ 1274 Golf Club Rd, Las Cruces, NM 88011, USA

Military Honors Spring Training Conference & Competition

Honor Guard volunteers from across New Mexico are invited to attend the 17th Annual Military Honors Training Conference and Competition on Friday, April 24, 2026, at Sonoma Ranch Golf Course in Las Cruces. Hosted in partnership with the New Mexico Department of Veterans’ Services and the New Mexico National Guard, this biannual training strengthens the skills and precision required to render military funeral honors with dignity and professionalism. Participants will receive hands-on instruction from military funeral honors experts, take part in demonstrations and breakout sessions, and compete in team evaluations that include flag folding, presentation, sounding of Taps, and rifle volleys. NMDVS supports more than 30 all-volunteer Honor Guard teams across the state who provide final honors for veterans and ensure no one is laid to rest without the recognition they have earned. This training continues a longstanding commitment to uphold the highest standards of military funeral honors while honoring the service and sacrifice of New Mexico’s veterans.
